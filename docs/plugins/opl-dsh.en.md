Manage OPL Gateway accounts and models in official DeepSeek Harness, with first-run setup, enhancement updates and shared desktop features.

Open **Settings → OPL Gateway** for account and model-group access. Execution engines, combinations, role bindings, team integration and task feedback are unified in **@one-person-lab/dsh-opl**, while model providers and credentials stay in their respective owners.

Disabling the plugin retains saved account settings, combinations and session data. Official application resources and official plugins remain unchanged.

Maintained by **One Person Lab**. [Project](https://github.com/gaofeng21cn/opl-dsh)
